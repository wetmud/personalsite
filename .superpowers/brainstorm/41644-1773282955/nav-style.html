A — Floating pill nav

Centered pill that floats at the top of every section. Blurred glass effect. Minimal, modern. Sits above the content without being heavy.

B — Side dot nav

Vertical dots on the right edge. One dot per section. Active section is filled. Hover shows section label. Very common on full-screen scroll sites.
